## The brief

Show a luxury leather bag online the way it is seen in a store: up close, from every side, with the craft details explained.

## What I built

A real-time 3D product page: the bag floats under spotlights over a dark, rippling floor. Visitors switch between **front, back, side and inside** views with smooth camera moves, open hotspots for features such as the carry system, and go straight to the shop.
