## The brief

Sell limited-edition villas where luxury meets nature, before a buyer ever visits.

## What I built

A cinematic, scroll-driven site: it opens on a rendered approach to the villas, their green facades and gardens, then moves through the story (space, privacy, nature, luxury, connectivity), the surroundings, the amenities and the engineering behind the homes.
