## The brief

Give a fried-chicken brand a website that feels like stepping into its restaurant.

## What I built

A walk-through 3D site: the camera moves through the dining room under the brand's lit sign, past the self-order kiosks and serving robots, and into the open kitchen, with animated food moments and sound, alongside the usual story, locations, menu and careers pages.
