## The brief

Take a Coimbatore computer store online, with a catalogue large enough for serious PC builders and gamers.

## What I built

A complete storefront and back office: categories for laptops, processors, graphics cards, monitors, printers and motherboards; **instant search** with product suggestions; **filters by spec** such as series, chipset and memory type; detailed product pages with full specifications, delivery checks and **EMI options**; offers and new arrivals; and a **custom PC builder**.
