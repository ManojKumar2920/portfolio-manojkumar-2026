## The brief

Make solar, batteries and EV charging easy to picture for homeowners, for WA's top-rated solar installer (45,000+ installations since 2003).

## What I built

A 3D hero for the home page: a cut-away house in its garden, with the camera flying over the solar array, into the rooms and past the EV in the driveway, leading into the reviews, awards and products.
